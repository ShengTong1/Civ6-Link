notes
- This Relay sees PLAINTEXT: the F3 prototype has no encryption (design section 4.1).
- It does not decrypt, rewrite, retransmit or reorder; one input envelope produces at
most one output datagram, and the payload bytes are copied unchanged.

beyond loopback testing, no L1/L2 Noise keys (those are M0-B).
